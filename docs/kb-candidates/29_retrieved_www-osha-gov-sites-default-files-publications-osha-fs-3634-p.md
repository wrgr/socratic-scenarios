Safety Guidance** * **General Hazards & Exposure Routes:** * Workers using nanotechnology may be exposed to nanomaterials through inhalation, skin contact, or ingestion. * The potential for nanomaterials to pose health or safety hazards is greater if they are easily dispersed (e.g., in powders, sprays, droplets) or not isolated/contained. * **Identified Health Hazards:** * Certain inhaled nanoparticles may deposit in the respiratory tract, causing inflammation and damage to lung cells and tissues (e.g., carbon nanotubes, nanofibers causing pulmonary inflammation and fibrosis). * Nanoscale Titanium dioxide (TiO₂) particles have higher mass-based potency than larger particles and are considered a potential occupational carcinogen by inhalation. * Certain nanoparticles may penetrate cell membranes and damage intracellular structures/cellular functions. * **Identified Chemical/Physical Hazards:** * Some nanomaterials can act as chemical catalysts, potentially causing unanticipated reactions, explosions, and fires. * Some nanoparticle dusts may be combustible and require less energy to ignite than larger dust particles, increasing explosion and fire risks. * **Employer Responsibilities & Worker Training:** * Employers should provide information and training to workers exposed to nanomaterials. * Training must include: * Identification of nanomaterials used and processes involved. * Results of any workplace exposure assessments. * Identification of engineering, administrative controls, and PPE to reduce exposure. * Proper use and limitations of PPE. * Emergency measures for nanomaterial spills or releases. * **Worker Rights (as safety guidance for workers):** * Right to working conditions that do not pose a risk of serious harm. * Right to receive information and training about workplace hazards, prevention methods, and applicable OSHA standards, in an understandable language. * Right to receive copies of work-related injury/illness records. * Right to receive copies of results from tests and monitoring of workplace hazards. * Right to exercise safety rights without retaliation. **II.
