Diagnostics * **Definitions of Rework, Modification, and Repair:** Understanding these terms is crucial for diagnosing the *type* of work needed and the appropriate controls. * **Rework:** Reprocessing noncomplying articles to assure *full compliance* with original specifications. * **Modification:** Revising functional capability to satisfy *new acceptance criteria*, typically driven by design changes. * **Repair:** Restoring functional capability of a defective article in a manner that *does not assure full compliance* with original drawings/specifications. * **Material Review Board (MRB):** For defects discovered during manufacturing, an MRB is typically responsible for dispositioning whether to rework, repair, use "as is," or discard. This is a critical diagnostic and decision-making step. * **Level of Conformance:** This serves as a diagnostic measure for the *outcome* of a procedure. A procedure is successful if it meets the stated Level of Conformance (High, Medium, Low) for its application. * **Visual Inspection:** Implied by "additional inspection guidelines" (1.1) and the focus on "visual product requirements" within the Level of Conformance. * **Functional Testing:** Implied by "restoring the functional capability" (Repair) and "minimum impact on end use functionality or reliability" (1.3). --- ### Safety Guidance * **Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) and Electrical Overstress (EOS) Controls (1.8.1):** Essential for preventing damage to sensitive electronic components.
